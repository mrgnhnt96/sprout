EVERY QUIET SWEEP CARRIES ITS OWN WHY, so a watchdog that is silent because it is
healthy is distinguishable from one that is silent because it died:

  - rang for 1 of 1 node(s): mtko31vt-ed147022 stalled
  - no ring: 1 node(s) still contradicted but are at the ring cap of 3 (mtko31vt-ed147022) — silenced until each advances, not silenced for good
